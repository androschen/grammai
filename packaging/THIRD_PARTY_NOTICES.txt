GrammAI includes Python, Qt for Python (PySide6), NumPy, SoundCard,
faster-whisper, CTranslate2, ONNX Runtime, Transformers, Hugging Face Hub,
SentencePiece, tokenizers, PyThaiNLP, pypinyin, PyAV, psutil and dependencies.
Distribution metadata and license files are included in the application bundle.

Qt for Python: https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/licenses.html
Python: https://docs.python.org/3/license.html
The application uses dynamically linked Qt libraries in _internal/PySide6.
Corresponding Qt source: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/

Model weights and external Vulkan servers are not bundled in this installer.
They retain their own licenses and are downloaded/configured separately.
NLLB weights: https://huggingface.co/facebook/nllb-200-distilled-600M
Qwen weights: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-GGUF
Whisper weights: https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp
